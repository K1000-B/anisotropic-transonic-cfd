The normative source is [Theory_Latex/main.tex](Theory_Latex/main.tex), introduced by commit `e30df92`. Its three inputs under [Theory_Latex/figures](Theory_Latex/figures) are illustrations. Coordinates use one consistent physical or nondimensional length scale. Density is a scalar at each vertex, with curvature in density per length squared. ## Interpretation and numerical equivalences

- The $L^p$ objective introduces $p\ge1$. The boxed optimization problem explicitly fixes $p\in\mathbb{N}^*$, which this implementation enforces.
- The text introducing `eq:bounded_metric_complexity` cites `eq:discrete_complexity` for the definition of $A_i$. The weights are defined in `eq:lumped_nodal_area`.
- The exact connectivity identity is $T=2V-B-2\chi$ for planar triangulations with disjoint manifold boundary loops, with $B$ boundary vertices and $\chi=V-E+T$. Mission 01 has $\chi=0$ and $B=606$, giving $T=13{,}702$ for $V=7{,}154$. The default conversion in MA-C01 follows the source approximation, including for a domain with a hole.
- Spectral logarithms use $\log\det K_i=\log k_{1,i}+\log k_{2,i}$. Bisection in $\log\beta$ preserves monotonicity and brackets full saturation at both ends. It clips the scaled optimal spectrum at each evaluation. The bounded field satisfies the nodal budget under the adopted quadrature. Its continuous integral may differ, and its relation to the original variational optimum changes when limits bind.
- Along each source-face interval the directional quadratic form is affine. For endpoint square roots $a,b>0$, the mean square root is $2(a^2+ab+b^2)/(3(a+b))$, including the constant limit. The implementation integrates each interval and sums the results. Round-off near a shared edge or boundary is handled by projection onto the closed barycentric simplex within the location allowance. A resolvable exterior interval raises an error.
- The double P1 recovery can be biased near the boundary and on irregular triangulations. A regularised tanh transition has zero exact curvature at its inflection. Its strongest curvature lies to either side, and finite reconstruction stencils can shift or broaden those peaks. The reference pipeline applies the curvature floor with no extra sensor.
